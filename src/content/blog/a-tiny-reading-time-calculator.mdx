## Calculate the estimate

Divide the word count by the reading pace and round up. At 225 words per minute, a 900-word draft takes about four minutes.

```js
const minutes = Math.ceil(wordCount / wordsPerMinute);
```

Keep the inputs in an Astro component so the post can explain the calculation in prose. The surrounding article is ordinary Markdown.
