I wanted a searchable directory of bookmarks. I ended up making a single HTML page with links to the ten pages I look up the most. The page solved the part of the problem I kept running into: I could remember the topic, but rarely the title or URL.

## Pick the part you keep repeating

Start with one routine you can describe in a sentence. Mine was opening a fresh browser tab and looking up the same set of reference pages. That gave me a small enough project to make in one sitting.

## Stop when it works in your routine

Put the result somewhere you'll naturally find it the next day. After a week of opening the page, I knew which bookmarks deserved labels and where search might help. That was a better basis for the next edit than my original list of features.
